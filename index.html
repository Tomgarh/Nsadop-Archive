Remembering Where We Come From

The NSADOP Archive is a digital home for the history, people, culture and memories of our community.

From our elders and political leaders to teachers, scholars, families and community builders, this archive seeks to preserve the stories that shaped who we are.

Every photograph, document and story added to this archive becomes part of a legacy for the next generation.

"Our people are the pages of our history. When we preserve their stories, we preserve ourselves."

A Legacy Worth Remembering

The Story of Our People

The history of Nsadep is carried through the lives of the people who served, taught, built, led and inspired generations.

This archive begins with their stories and will grow as more members of the community contribute memories, photographs and historical records.

1930

Birth of Chief Michael Ogon Asu

Born on 10 October 1930 and later becoming one of the prominent political figures associated with Nsadep and Boki.

1938

Birth of Professor Kevin Michael Ogon Etta

Born on 13 May 1938 and later becoming the first Boki professor and a pioneering university administrator.

2021

Professor Emmanuel Odu Obi

Elected Dean of the Faculty of Physical Science in 2021, according to the historical account provided for this archive.
